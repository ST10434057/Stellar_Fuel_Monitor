### F1 — Headline daily average included Data Warning cycles — **FIXED**

`daily().averageKmL` averaged every finite `analysedKmL`, including cycles the engine itself
rated `Data Warning` and excluded from all benchmarks. A handful of capture errors could
therefore drag the dashboard headline to a figure no truck in the fleet achieves.

- Operating dates that would be affected without the filter: **562 of 969**
- Latest complete date 2026-09-03: now reports **2.4** km/L; unfiltered it read **12.13** km/L
- Fixed in `cycle-analytics.js` `daily()`; the dead value-matching `valid` filter above it
  (which resolved ratings by floating-point km/L and could match the wrong cycle) was removed.
- Locked by `server/test/cycle-analytics.test.js` — "the daily view keeps data warnings out of
  the review queue and the average".

### F4 — `excludedOutliers` is always 0 on the V20 engine

The full-cycle engine performs no MAD/IQR outlier rejection (only the wide 0.20–6.50 km/L
physical guardrail). `publicBenchmark` hard-codes `excludedOutliers: 0`, yet the Android
`TripBenchmark` DTO carries it as if it were a computed statistic. The demonstration must not
claim outlier removal on this engine.

### F5 — The Destinations count double-counts routes that share one benchmark pool

Benchmarking keys on the normalised `routeKey`, but `destinationsForVehicle()` keys on the
raw destination string. Case and spelling variants of one place are therefore one pool but
several rows in the vehicle Destinations count.

- Vehicles where the Destinations count exceeds the distinct route count: **96 of 129**
- Worked example: LZ39MYGP shows **60** destinations but has **50** distinct routes
  — the labels `BOOYSENDAL` (99), `Booysendal Mine` (40), `booysendal` (3), `Booysendal` (1) all normalise to `DEST:BOOYSENDAL` and share one benchmark pool
- Benchmarks are unaffected — only the displayed destination count is inflated.
